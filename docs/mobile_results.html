Investment Eco-System - results on your phone

Five stocks analysed against each other and the S&P 500, plus the Coca-Cola study. Every number was produced by the app's own OLS and logistic regression code.

1 - Growth of 100 (log scale)

All six rebased to 100

Log axis: equal vertical distance = equal percentage move. NVIDIA ends near 1,100; Coca-Cola near 178.

2 - Market model

StockBetaR2 Vol5y

An 8x spread in market sensitivity. NVIDIA moves 2.17% for every 1% of the market; Coca-Cola moves 0.26%. No stock here shows statistically defensible alpha once you correct for testing five of them at once.

3 - Correlation of daily returns

Coca-Cola and NVIDIA correlate -0.02 - the most defensive and most aggressive names are effectively independent. That is the real diversification in this set.

4 - Coca-Cola's beta collapsed

Rolling 120-day beta of KO to the S&P 500

A single "beta" number hides this completely. It swings from strongly positive to negative.

Split chronologically, the halves disagree: beta +0.389 (p=3e-21) on the training period, then -0.018 (p=0.87) on held-out data. The logistic classifier went from 0.686 AUC in-sample to 0.436 out-of-sample - worse than a coin flip.

5 - Which models actually generalise

StockAUC inAUC out Verdict

The market R2 predicts it in advance. The three stocks the market explains well (Apple, NVIDIA, JPMorgan) hold up out-of-sample. Coca-Cola and ExxonMobil - the two with the lowest R2 - collapse to a coin flip.
